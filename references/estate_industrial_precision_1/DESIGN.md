## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for precision industrial agriculture and batch manufacturing environments. It serves estate superintendents, factory chemical engineers, and tea manufacturing masters monitoring critical leaf withering, moisture evaporation, air volume, and drying bays across high-elevation tea factories in Sri Lanka.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Precision Industrial IoT** with a **Refined Botanical Heritage**. The interface rejects cluttered legacy SCADA systems in favor of an airy, modern supervisory control dashboard. High data density is managed through rigorous typographic hierarchy, purposeful status signaling, and calming forest hues that reduce visual fatigue during continuous 24-hour shifts.

Key attributes:
- **Calm Authority**: Critical operational metrics are front and center without excessive visual noise or garish alarms.
- **Agricultural Precision**: Deep canopy greens meet clean engineered surfaces, invoking high-altitude agro-climatic control.
- **Immediate Legibility**: Real-time ambient sensors, CFM airflow rates, hygrometric depressions, and heat exchanger states can be audited at a glance under varied field and control-room lighting conditions.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-throughput factory telemetry in deep natural greens and crisp architectural neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand Tier**: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) anchors key interactive states, top-level navigation, primary command triggers, and high-emphasis active filters. Supporting Deep Green (`#2D6A4F`) provides structural hover and sub-surface emphasis.
- **Secondary & Accent Tier**: Ceylon Tea Emerald (`#40916C`) and Lively Emerald (`#52B788`) serve as focus indicators, active progress bars, continuous sensor telemetry lines, and interactive toggles.
- **Backgrounds & Canvases**: 
  - Canvas Root: Light Mist Off-White (`#F4F6F4` / `#F8F9FA`) ensures separation from structural cards.
  - Surface Elevation 0 (Containers & Bay Modules): Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Subtle Dividing Line: Refined Slate Border (`#E2E8F0`).

### Semantic Status Signaling
Color communicates operational thresholds strictly:
- **Optimal / Nominal Flow**: Forest Sage (`#2E7D32`) on delicate mist container (`#E8F5E9`).
- **Attention / Moisture Deviation**: Warm Ceylon Ochre (`#D97706`) on light amber fill (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Critical / Bay Overheat Alarms**: Terracotta Crimson (`#DC2626`) on soft rose field (`#FEE2E2`).
- **Offline / Stale Sensor Node**: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) on cool slate neutral (`#F1F5F9`).

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering wide counters, clear vertical stroke weights, and contemporary geometric stability.

### Telemetry & Tabular Numerics
To prevent jitter and alignment drift in fast-updating sensor feeds (temperatures in °C, moisture percentages, relative humidity, and CFM rates), all metric displays and numeric tables must activate tabular figures via CSS (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums;`).

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Metric Display (`metric-display`)**: Dedicated to vital bay readings (e.g., `28.4 °C`, `64.2% RH`). Paired with `metric-sub` for baseline deltas and sensor identifiers.
- **Section Titles**: Scaled down slightly to prioritize density and dashboard overview without sacrificing scanning speed.
- **Labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`)**: Used for sensor statuses, valve states, batch run times, and metadata keys.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is an adaptive fluid grid structured for 24-bay factory trough arrays, heat exchangers, and multi-deck monitoring consoles.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Margin: `1rem`, Gutter: `0.75rem`. Cards reflow into vertical operational feeds.
- **Tablet / Industrial Pad (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout. Margin: `1.5rem`, Gutter: `1rem`. Accommodates side-by-side bay comparisons and zone heatmaps.
- **Desktop / Control Desk (≥ 1200px)**: 12-column layout. Margin: `2rem`, Gutter: `1.5rem`. Enables persistent plant-overview navigation, active bay matrix, and live alert telemetry panels.

### Spacing Philosophy
A 4px baseline grid anchors structural rhythms:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled sensor readouts, gauge ticks, and icon-to-metric pairings.
- Use `space-md` (12px) for card body internal padding and cell separations.
- Use `space-lg` (20px) for container padding across monitoring modules.
- Use `space-xl` (32px) for major operational section divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy shadows, which introduce optical mud in bright factory floor lighting. Instead, depth is achieved through **low-contrast micro-borders coupled with soft ambient diffusion**.

### Layering Levels
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#F4F6F4`. No shadow, zero elevation.
- **Bay Tile / Dashboard Card**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. Elevation shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.02)`.
- **Active / Focused Bay Monitor**: Border elevates to 1px `#40916C` or state color, backed by a subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Modal / Diagnostic Drawer**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border 1px `#E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced curvature scale (`roundedness: 2`) that brings soft organic warmth into industrial equipment interfaces while preserving architectural structure.

### Radius Scale Implementation
- **Micro UI Elements (Pills, Status Badges, Sensor Dots)**: `rounded-full` (9999px) for clear badge recognition.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, Dropdowns**: `0.5rem` (8px) for crisp hand targets on ruggedized factory tablets.
- **Withering Bay Cards, Metric Panels**: `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for distinct module definition.
- **Modal Dialogs & Large Overlays**: `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Initiate Fan Reversal", "Commit Batch")**: Solid `#1B4332` background, white label, subtle hover transition to `#2D6A4F`. 8px border-radius, minimum 40px touch-target height (44px on tablet).
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Calibrate Probe", "Export Log")**: Crisp pure white fill, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, text in `#1B4332`. Hover: `#F4F6F4` fill with `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Destructive / Emergency Stop**: Solid `#DC2626` background, white text, active press with high-contrast ring.

### Metric Cards & Withering Bay Tiles
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `16px` border-radius.
- **Header**: Contains bay identifier (e.g., "Trough Bay 04 - High Elevation Batch #109"), operation phase badge, and run-time timer.
- **Body**: Grid of dual primary gauges (Dry Bulb °C / Wet Bulb °C) alongside relative humidity percentage and CFM air throughput. Numeric values use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`.
- **Status Accent**: 3px vertical accent bar on the card's left margin using the semantic status color (Green, Amber, Crimson, Slate).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Formed using full-pill rounding (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Include a 6px circular dot indicator on the leading edge.
- Example: **Optimal** uses `#E8F5E9` background, `#2E7D32` text and dot; **Over-Temperature Alert** uses `#FEE2E2` background, `#DC2626` text and dot.

### Sensor Inputs & Bay Setpoint Controls
- High-contrast form elements with 8px radius, white surface, and 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Focus state reveals a 2px outer glow in `#40916C` at 25% opacity with `#2D6A4F` outline.
- Built with stepper increment/decrement handles sized for single-finger adjustment in high-moisture factory floors.

### Data Tables (Batch Schedules & Loggers)
- Row heights set to `44px` for compact scan density.
- Header row styled in `#F8F9FA` with uppercase `label-sm` text in `#64748B`.
- Zebra striping is avoided in favor of 1px horizontal dividers (`#F1F5F9`) and immediate `#F4F6F4` row hover states.
