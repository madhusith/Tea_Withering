## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for precision agro-industrial supervisory systems, leaf withering bays, and batch processing automation. It balances rugged agricultural engineering with contemporary industrial IoT clarity.

Built for estate superintendents, factory floor engineers, and telemetry analysts, the system pairs deep plantation forest greens with corporate engineering blues to communicate stability, mechanical rigor, and scientific precision.

### Key Attributes
- **Agro-Industrial Authority**: Deep Ceylon tea plantation forest greens anchor primary governance, while structured corporate navy and cobalt blues drive telemetry channels and digital instrumentation.
- **Operator Legibility**: Eliminates dense legacy SCADA visual clutter in favor of high-legibility typographic hierarchies, tabular metric alignment, and clear spatial separation.
- **Instrumental Restraint**: Color is applied with tactical precision—reserving saturated cobalt and cyan tones for live state tracking, active telemetry feeds, and interactive control triggers.

## Colors

The color architecture bridges natural plantation heritage with industrial control engineering. The primary brand palette anchors on high-elevation tea leaf green, accented and partitioned by Hayleys corporate blues for telemetry, automation status, and analytics.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#1b4332`)**: Used for core structural boundaries, primary buttons, system headers, and authoritative navigation bars.
- **Secondary (`#0a369d`)**: Drives secondary control actions, instrument selection rails, diagnostic panels, and batch configuration controls.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7`)**: Applied strictly to dynamic telemetry indicators, real-time sensor line charts, live state badges, and high-frequency digital readouts.
- **Neutral (`#0b1c30`)**: Deep slate navy serving as the foundational ink color for maximum contrast and legibility across all ambient lighting conditions.

### Named Colors & Semantics
- **Hayleys Navy (`#0a369d`) & Deep Navy (`#1e3a8a`)**: Establish secondary interactive hierarchy, table header categorizations, and auxiliary panel framing.
- **Cobalt Blue (`#0056b3`) & Telemetry Cyan (`#0284c7`)**: Signal live runtime data streams, flow rates (CFM), fan rotational state flags, and real-time chart strokes.
- **Status Signals**:
  - `status-nominal` (`#2e7d32`): Moisture and temperature ranges within batch envelope.
  - `status-warning` (`#d97706`): Hygrometric depression anomaly, airflow imbalance.
  - `status-critical` (`#dc2626`): Withering trough overheat, furnace flame failure, emergency cut-offs.

## Typography

The type system is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering wide counters, clear vertical stroke weights, and contemporary geometric stability across varied factory lighting conditions.

### Tabular Telemetry Rule
All dynamic numbers, sensor values, run clocks, and status tables must enforce tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums;`). This prevents horizontal jitter as values change rapidly in real time.

### Role Applications
- **`metric-display`**: Dedicated to primary physical telemetry readouts (e.g., dry bulb °C, humidity %, CFM throughput).
- **`metric-sub`**: Supporting sensor metadata, baseline deviations, and secondary units.
- **`label-sm` & `label-md`**: Uppercase tracking for field-hardware tags, relay codes, and telemetry badges.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are based on a responsive fluid grid designed to fit high-density telemetry displays, supervisory multi-bay screens, and rugged field tablets.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin` (1rem / 16px), `gutter` (1rem / 16px). Stacked bay feeds and vertical telemetry strips.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1rem / 16px). Side-by-side trough diagnostics and twin-fan balance metrics.
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (2rem / 32px), `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem / 24px). Multi-bay matrix overview, real-time drying graphs, and persistent alert streams.

### Spacing Principles
- Compact micro-spacings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) bind measurement labels directly to their corresponding metric figures.
- Mid-range spacings (`space-md`, `space-lg`) isolate distinct physical trough modules and sensor groups.
- Maximum spacing (`space-xl`) designates batch workflow transitions and plant section dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

To guarantee legibility under harsh, high-glare industrial lighting, this system utilizes structural tonal surfaces and low-contrast borders instead of heavy drop shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas**: Neutral tint surface (`#f8f9ff`). Flat, 0px elevation.
- **Standard Card Surface**: Clean white (`#ffffff`) bounded by a 1px solid border (`#c1c8c2` at 50% opacity). Minimal ground shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.05)`.
- **Selected / Active Telemetry Module**: Surface remains `#ffffff`, bordered by 1.5px solid corporate blue (`#0a369d`) or forest green (`#1b4332`), paired with a focused ambient tint: `0 4px 14px rgba(10, 54, 157, 0.08)`.
- **Overlays & Command Drawers**: Elevated `#ffffff` container with 1px border (`#1e3a8a` at 20% opacity) and directional shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a structured, balanced radius scale (`roundedness: 2`) that brings ergonomic precision without appearing excessively playful.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Badges, Status Dots, Telemetry Pills**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) for quick scanning.
- **Buttons, Steppers, Inputs, Control Chips**: `0.5rem` (8px) for confident touch-target boundaries on industrial tablets.
- **Telemetry Bays, Machine Tiles, Chart Cards**: `1rem` (16px) to clearly frame discrete factory modules.
- **Modal Dialogs & System Drawers**: `1.5rem` (24px) to separate system-level commands from underlying telemetry grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Command (Batch Launch, Fan Start)**: Solid plantation forest green (`#1b4332`) with white text. Hover transitions to `#012d1d`.
- **Secondary Action (Telemetry Filter, Setpoint Calibrate)**: Clean white surface with 1.5px border in Hayleys navy (`#0a369d`), text in `#0a369d`. Hover shifts to `#eff4ff`.
- **Tertiary / Telemetry Toggle**: Subtle background `#eff4ff`, text and icon in cobalt blue (`#0056b3`), border-free.
- **Emergency / Cut-off**: High-visibility crimson (`#dc2626`) fill, white text, 2px focus ring.

### Telemetry Badges & Status Chips
- Height: 24px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), internal horizontal padding `space-sm`.
- **Active Channel**: Telemetry cyan background (`#0284c7` at 15% opacity), text in `#0056b3`, leading 6px pulsing dot in `#0284c7`.
- **Nominal State**: Light sage container, text in `#2e7d32`, 6px green indicator dot.
- **Deviation / Critical State**: Soft amber or red container with corresponding deep warning text and steady indicator dot.

### Withering Bay Metric Cards
- White background, 16px corner radius, framed by a 1px border in `#c1c8c2`.
- Left-side operational indicator: 4px vertical border accent using `#1b4332` for standard running, `#0284c7` for active monitoring, or `#dc2626` for alarm state.
- Numeric layout: `metric-display` in tabular figures, accompanied by `metric-sub` in uppercase `label-sm` for units and sensor addresses.

### Inputs & Actuator Steppers
- Height: 44px for field-glove compatibility.
- 1px border in `#c1c8c2`, white background. Focused state renders a 2px outline in `#0a369d` with zero offset blur.
- Dual-ended stepper buttons for damper position and burner temperature setpoints with distinct active-press feedback.

### Telemetry Charts & Trend Lines
- Chart background: `#ffffff` with horizontal gridlines in `#eff4ff`.
- Primary sensor line: Ceylon Forest Green (`#1b4332`) at 2.5px width.
- Secondary telemetry stream (Airflow / CFM): Corporate Cobalt Blue (`#0056b3`) at 2px width.
- Target setpoint baseline: Dashed line in Hayleys Navy (`#0a369d` at 50% opacity).

### Data Tables & Event Logs
- Compact 40px row height for high visual density.
- Header row styled in `#f8f9ff` with uppercase `label-sm` in deep navy (`#1e3a8a`).
- Row borders: 1px horizontal line in `#eff4ff`. Hover state applies `#eff4ff` at 60% opacity across the full row.
